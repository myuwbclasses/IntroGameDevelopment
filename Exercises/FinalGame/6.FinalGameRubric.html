About this milestone

Expect to see
  • A complete, polished game
  • Splash/title screen, menus and HUD
  • Win/lose screens, audio and credits
  • Difficulty levels or other progression

How to score

  • Enter one whole number for each category, from 5 (best) to 1.
  • When a category has more than one criterion, weigh them together. If you're unsure, average them and round to the nearest whole number.
  • The descriptions are anchors, not checklists: pick the column that fits best.
  • Score what you saw today, not what the team says it will add later.
  • Most criteria are subjective. Apply your own judgment, but apply it the same way to every team.

Hints on preparation

Before the final evaluation game play, once again, you have a chance to tell us something interesting and direct our attention. Again, choose what and how much to say wisely.

Guide: you can include

Goal: why the world should think your game is the best!

  • Changes made because of playtest feedback
  • A summary of your efforts
  • Cool or special features
  • What was most challenging, and what was easy
  • Maybe a slide on the game's commands

Don't talk at length: keep it short.

Examples from the previous year (2017)

  • Six God
    • Time spent
    • Cool features (slides 3 and 4)
    • Last slide: the controls (leave this on while everyone plays)
  • Snake

    • But: a little long
  • Pirate
