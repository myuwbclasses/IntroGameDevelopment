Game Pitch Rubric

The team proposes its game: rules, what makes it interesting, how hard it will be to build, and what it will prototype first.

About this milestone

Expect to see
  • The goal of the game, and how you win and lose
  • The core mechanics and controls
  • What makes it different from existing games
  • The expected technical difficulties, and the prototypes that will test them
Not expected yet
  • Working software
  • Final art or audio

How to score

  • Enter one whole number for each category, from 5 (best) to 1.
  • When a category has more than one criterion, weigh them together. If you're unsure, average them and round to the nearest whole number.
  • The descriptions are anchors, not checklists: pick the column that fits best.
  • Score what you saw today, not what the team says it will add later.
  • Most criteria are subjective. Apply your own judgment, but apply it the same way to every team.

Hints on preparation

Guide: you should include

  • Two sentences on your game that define its spirit
  • Plot, objective (win/lose conditions), rules, characters (hero and enemies) and items
  • Sketches of:
    • the UI
    • one game level
    • the style
  • Digital prototypes to test out (very important):
    • Identify what will be difficult for you
    • Prototypes should be small, well tested and well abstracted
