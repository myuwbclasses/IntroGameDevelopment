About this milestone

Expect to see
  • Changes made since the alpha playtest
  • The game close to its final form
Not expected yet
  • Polish
  • Final art or audio (rough is okay)
  • Perfect stability (an occasional crash is okay)

How to score

  • Enter one whole number for each category, from 5 (best) to 1.
  • When a category has more than one criterion, weigh them together. If you're unsure, average them and round to the nearest whole number.
  • The descriptions are anchors, not checklists: pick the column that fits best.
  • Score what you saw today, not what the team says it will add later.
  • Most criteria are subjective. Apply your own judgment, but apply it the same way to every team.

Written feedback

Along with your scores, post short, clear, polite feedback using the feedback template: start with what works, then name the most important things that don't, with a suggestion for each.

While you play, the playtest questions can help you notice what to comment on: rules, controls, interface, and the moments that were fun or frustrating.

Hints on preparation

During each playtest (alpha and beta), before we begin playing your game, you have a chance to tell us what we should pay attention to.

Watch out!Playtesting time is short: every minute you spend presenting is a minute less for us to test your game! :)

Guide: you can include

Goal: to direct your testers' attention.

  • 1 slide to remind everyone what your game is about (a screenshot from the demo works well here)
  • 1 slide to explain what information you are looking for, for example:
    • How does it feel when the hero jumps?
    • Which of the mechanics do you prefer (with double jump or without)?
    • Does “sliding on ice” feel right?
  • Possibly a slide showing the game controls
Do
  • Remember, the goal is to remind us what your game is about and show us the game
  • Keep the presentation short
  • Include your own survey questions, if you like: no more than 4–6 questions, with easy access (e.g., the QR code from Six God)
Don't
  • Talk at length (the important part is the demo)
